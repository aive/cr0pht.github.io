02 — Approach

From zero to compounding.

Every engagement follows the same loop. Small enough to start next week, rigorous enough to scale.

  1. 0

    Diagnose

    Two weeks inside your data, product and team. We leave with a clear view of what's working, what isn't, and the three bets most likely to pay off.

  2. 1

    Build

    A senior squad ships the first version fast: real software, real users, real numbers. No twelve-month roadmaps before anyone has touched the thing.

  3. 2

    Measure

    Everything is instrumented from day one. Weekly reviews against the metrics that matter to the business, not vanity dashboards.

  4. 3

    Scale

    Double down on what works, kill what doesn't, and hand over a system your own team can run. We measure success by how little you need us afterwards.
